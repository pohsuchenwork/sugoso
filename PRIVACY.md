# Privacy Notice

> **Plain-language notice - not legal advice.** This notice is accurate **while the app stays local and makes no network calls of its own** (verified in the source). If the app's data practices ever change, it must be updated, and the operator should have a privacy-qualified lawyer confirm it for their jurisdiction.

**Product:** Sugoso - a macOS menu-bar focus timer.
**Last updated:** 2026-06-10

## The short version
Sugoso runs entirely on your Mac. **It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers operated by the Licensor.

## What the app handles
- **Your settings** - focus/break durations and on/off toggles (notifications, sound, break overlay) are stored locally on your device using macOS preferences (`UserDefaults`). They never leave your device and are never sent to the Licensor or anyone else.
- **Your tasks** - any tasks you create (their names, your pomodoro estimates, and completion counts) are stored locally on your device in the same way. Task names are free text that you type, so treat them like any note kept on your device. They never leave your device and are never sent to the Licensor or anyone else.
- **No personal data collected by us** - the app does not request, collect, transmit, or process names, email addresses, location, contacts, files, usage analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers. Anything you type (such as a task name) stays on your device; the Licensor never receives it.
- **No network communication** - the app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and does not connect to any server operated by the Licensor. The app may provide a link that, if you click it, opens your default browser to the Licensor's website; the app itself makes no network request, and that website has its own terms and privacy practices.

## Operating-system features
- **Notifications.** With your permission, the app asks macOS to display local notifications. That permission and the notifications are handled by macOS; no notification content is sent to the Licensor. Apple's handling of system features is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Launch at login (optional).** If you enable auto-start, a standard macOS LaunchAgent file is created locally on your device. No data is transmitted.

## Cookies and tracking
None. The app is not a website and uses no cookies, web beacons, or cross-app tracking.

## Children
The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar)
Because the Licensor does not collect or process your personal data, there is generally no personal data held by the Licensor to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict. Any settings stored on your device are under your control and are removed when you delete the app and its preferences.
